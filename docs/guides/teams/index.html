Teams

Teams are the unit of sharing in YAPAT. Datasets, label spaces, and annotations belong to a team, and membership determines what a user can see and do.

Roles

Role Scope
Admin Platform-wide (global role). Sees all datasets and teams, and in the interface creates datasets and teams and dataset invitation links.
Team owner Global role team_owner; also a per-team membership role. Manages a team: its members, invitations, and active label space, and can promote label-space versions and delete the team.
User A team member. Annotates the datasets the team can access.

Creating a team

The Teams page offers team creation to administrators, who may assign available datasets at creation. A dataset can belong to only one team; datasets already assigned elsewhere appear disabled. The backend also accepts team creation from other authenticated users. When the creator is not an administrator, they become the team's owner; an administrator who creates a team does not become its owner.

Member management

Opening a team shows its details and members. From here an owner can review members with their roles and join dates and invite more people. The backend has no route for renaming a team or changing its description.

Invitations

Invitation links attach a new account to a team or to datasets. They are not required for registration, which is also available on the sign-up page. Team invitations are created by a team owner or an administrator and expire after two days by default. A link carries a token and, for team invitations, an intended role:

  • Team owner invitation — the role stored in the link is owner. Registering through it makes the new user an owner of the team.
  • Team member invitation — the role stored in the link is user. Registering through it joins the team as a member.
  • Dataset invitation — created by an administrator, grants a user access to specific datasets.

A user who registers with an invitation is linked to the corresponding team or datasets. Invitation links can be deleted to revoke them.

Deleting a team

Administrators and team owners can delete a team. Deleting it unassigns its datasets — they are not deleted — while removing memberships and invitations. Users who had access through the team lose it. This action is irreversible.

Access checks. The backend verifies team membership and dataset access on the operations that need them; administrators bypass team-level checks. See REST API for the endpoint-level rules.

Teams

A team is a group of people who share datasets and label spaces. You see the datasets of the teams you belong to.

You need: an administrator account to create teams and invite people.

Roles

RoleCan
AdministratorSee all teams and datasets, add datasets, generate embeddings, create and delete teams, invite people, and view Study Logs.
Team ownerAnnotate, start a cold-start model, and change a dataset's spectrogram settings.
AnnotatorAnnotate the datasets of their teams.

Create a team

  1. Open Team Management on the Dashboard.
  2. Click Create Team.
  3. Enter a name and choose at least one dataset. A dataset belongs to one team only, so datasets already in a team are greyed out.
  4. Click Create.

Invite people

  1. In the team list, click Invite People for the team, then Copy link.
  2. Send the link. It is valid for two days and can be used more than once.
  3. The person opens it, registers an account, and joins the team.

The first link for a team without an owner makes the person its team owner. Later links add annotators. People who sign up on their own, without a link, get an annotator account that belongs to no team yet.

Delete a team

Click the delete icon next to the team in the team list. It acts immediately, with no confirmation. The datasets are kept but no longer belong to a team, and the team's members lose access through it.

Good to know. Team membership is managed from the team list. A team owner who already belongs to a team is taken to the team page, which shows "Team not found."; ask an administrator to invite people or change the team. Invitation links cannot be revoked; they expire after two days.
For developers.
  • Global roles are admin, team_owner, and user; a membership is owner or user.
  • Invitation links point to /login/?token=…&target_role=owner|user. Set VITE_YAPAT_FRONTEND_URL so links use the public address.
  • The team page (/teams/{id}) calls GET /api/teams/{id}, which the backend does not provide. See REST API.

Label Management

Label Management is where your team decides what to label. You describe the sounds you care about in plain language, the assistant finds matching entries in trusted sources such as the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF), and you keep the ones you want. The result is your team's label space.

You need: the ontology service running. See Installation.

Steps

  1. Open Label Management on the Dashboard. The page is titled Pre-Annotation. If you belong to several teams, choose the target team first.
  2. Describe what you want to label, for example "I want to annotate Panthera leo", and press Enter or Send. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  3. Review the suggested concepts and add the ones you want: Add All, or one at a time.
  4. Refine with more messages, and remove anything you added by mistake.
  5. Click Add To label space list to freeze the list.
  6. Click Label Space created, Start Annotating? to open the Annotation Hub.

The two panels

  • Taxonomy Assistant — the chat, where you talk with the assistant and see its suggestions.
  • Label Space — the list you are building, with the source of each concept and a search box.

After freezing

A frozen label space cannot be edited. Its concepts appear in the Annotation Hub's label list for everyone in the team. To change it, build and freeze a new one; the newest frozen label space is the one the Hub uses. See Freezing a Label Space.

For developers.
  • The page is at /pre-annotation; the chat API is under /api/taxonomy/chat.
  • Each message is forwarded to the oe_yapat service at OE_YAPAT_SERVICE_URL. See Grounding.

Label Management

Label Management is where your team decides what to label. You describe the sounds you care about in plain language, the assistant finds matching entries in trusted sources such as the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF), and you keep the ones you want. The result is your team's label space.

You need: the ontology service running. See Installation.

Steps

  1. Open Label Management on the Dashboard. The page is titled Pre-Annotation. Choose the Dataset at the top (administrators also choose the team).
  2. Describe what you want to label, for example "I want to annotate Panthera leo", and press Enter or Send. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  3. Review the suggested concepts and add the ones you want: Add All, or one at a time.
  4. Refine with more messages, and remove anything you added by mistake.
  5. Click Submit label space under the list to propose it as a new version. It then awaits the team owner's approval; once promoted, it becomes the active label space used for annotation.

The two panels

  • Taxonomy Assistant — the chat, where you talk with the assistant and see its suggestions.
  • Current Label Space — the list you are building. The count in the heading is the number of concepts, Active version names the version in use, and each concept shows its source (for example ENVO, WIKIPEDIA or LOCAL), a description and its identifier.

Quick labels, the short list annotators click in the Hub, are a separate list per dataset. See Quick labels.

After submitting

A submitted version is not edited. It waits for the team owner, who expands it under Label Space Versions and clicks Make active. The active version is the label space the team annotates with. To change it, build and submit a new list; earlier versions are kept. See Submitting a Label Space.

For developers.
  • The page is at /pre-annotation; the chat API is under /api/taxonomy/chat.
  • Each message is forwarded to the oe_yapat service at OE_YAPAT_SERVICE_URL. See Grounding.

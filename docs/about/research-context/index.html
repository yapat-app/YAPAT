YAPAT's label-space workflow builds on research at the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) and the University of Oldenburg.

Structuring Annotation Label Spaces by Natural Language Concept Elicitation and Ontology Grounding

P. Sitapara, P. Doddanawar, T. S. Gouvêa, and D. Sonntag. GenAIK-NORA 2026 workshop at IJCAI-ECAI 2026, Bremen.

Describes the approach behind Label Management: an expert names concepts in plain language, a language model looks them up in Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF), Environment Ontology (ENVO), and Wikipedia, and only confirmed concepts join the label space. Demonstration video · Code (oe_yapat)

Grounded Label Space Engineering for Knowledge-Centric Annotation Workflows

P. Sitapara, T. S. Gouvêa, and D. Sonntag. Challenge paper.

Argues that label spaces should be linked to shared knowledge resources and able to grow during annotation, instead of being fixed, flat lists of names. Proposes this as a research direction.

In YAPAT. These ideas appear as label spaces, grounding, and freezing. The papers describe research directions; these pages describe what the software does.

What is a Label Space?

A label space is your team's agreed list of things to label. Each entry is a concept linked to a trusted source, not just a word, so labels in different datasets can be compared by their identifiers.

Labels versus concepts

A plain label is only a name, and names are ambiguous: two teams may use different words for the same species, or the same word for different ones. A concept in YAPAT also stores an identifier from its source, such as gbif:2420576, and details like the scientific name, rank, family, and kingdom. The identifier makes labels comparable across datasets.

Where concepts come from

SourceUsed for
Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)Species and other taxa.
Environment Ontology (ENVO)Environmental sounds, such as rain or wind.
WikipediaA fallback when no ontology entry fits.
LocalYour own concept, when no source has one.

How a label space is made

  1. Describe what you want to label in the label-space chat.
  2. Keep the suggested concepts that fit. See Grounding.
  3. Freeze the list. Its concepts now appear in the Annotation Hub for your team. See Freezing a Label Space.

The list is flat: YAPAT does not store relations such as "is a kind of" between concepts.

For developers.
  • A frozen label space is stored as a custom taxonomy with status active. Statuses draft and archived exist in the model; archiving is API-only and hides the taxonomy from listings.
  • The taxonomy API covers GBIF and custom taxonomies: GET /api/taxonomy/available, /suggest, /search, /resolve. See REST API.

Freezing a Label Space

Freezing finishes a label space. The list becomes fixed, and its concepts appear in the Annotation Hub for everyone in your team.

How to freeze

In Label Management, click Add To label space list. The label space is saved as "Label space" followed by the date and time, and the conversation closes.

Changing a frozen label space

A frozen label space cannot be edited. To change it, build and freeze a new one. The Hub uses your team's newest frozen label space; earlier ones are kept.

What happens to existing labels

Nothing. Each annotation stores the concept's identifier and name from the moment it was made, so labels stay valid when the label space changes.

For developers. Freezing creates a custom taxonomy with status active, owned by the conversation's team and attributed to the user who froze it. There is no review or approval step, and a team can hold several active taxonomies. Annotations store taxon_id and resolved_name_snapshot, not a reference to the taxonomy.

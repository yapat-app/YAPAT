Filters and Model Scores

The Annotation Hub's sidebar has a Filters section that narrows the snippets of the active-learning feed and of the projection view, first by recording properties and, from study phase 4 on, by the model-derived scores. The feed can also be sorted by these values.

Available filters

Filter Values
Status All, Unlabeled, or Labeled snippets. For labelled snippets, a species selection narrows the list to snippets labelled with those species.
Location One or more recording locations.
Date, month, and time of day A date range, one or more months of the year, and a range of times of day, taken from the recording's date and time.
Model-derived scores Threshold sliders over histograms of uncertainty, diversity, density, confidence, and the composite score, with an optional selection of predicted species for the confidence score when Status is All.

When the filters are shown

The study phase decides which filters are visible. Phases 1 and 2 hide the sidebar. Phase 3 shows the status filter (with its species selection), and the location and date and time filters. Phases 4 and 5 add the model-derived scores. See the Annotation Hub guide.

A recording's location, date, and time come from its metadata, which is parsed from file names that follow a recognised convention or imported from a recording-metadata CSV on the Datasets page.

Model-derived scores

Uncertainty, diversity, density, and the composite score come with the model's predictions for each snippet. Confidence is computed in the browser from the model's predicted probabilities; when predicted species are selected, it measures how confidently the model detects any of them. The predicted labels themselves are never shown to the annotator.

Sorting the feed

The feed can be sorted by one or more properties: confidence, composite score, uncertainty, diversity, density, date, and time. In phase 3 the date and time sort options are shown but cannot be changed; in phases 4 and 5 all of them can be used.

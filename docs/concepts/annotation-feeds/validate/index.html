Validate

The user-study version has no validate annotation mode. The Annotation Hub has no mode selector, and the model's predicted labels are never shown for an annotator to confirm or reject.

What exists instead

The model's confidence is one of the model-derived scores. In study phases 4 and 5, annotators can filter and sort the feed by it in the Annotation Hub's sidebar, alongside uncertainty, diversity, density, and the composite score, and can narrow the confidence score to predicted species. The labels themselves are always chosen by the annotator.
